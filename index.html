About

I'm an Instructor I with MyComputerCareer, where I teach CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+ and CySA+ to veterans moving into technology careers, and mentor them through that move.

My service taught me to adapt quickly, solve problems with limited resources, and communicate clearly under pressure. Those are the same skills that carry a technician through a hard ticket, and they're what I build on with every student.

How does a student get past memorizing and into troubleshooting? With a steady foundation first, then complexity added one layer at a time, so every new idea is anchored to one they already own.

Foundation first. Then build.

That's how the fundamentals stop being trivia and start being skill.

The Cyber Warrior Program

The Cyber Warrior Program is the study platform I design for my students: more than 35 free, interactive study sites across A+, Network+, Security+ and CySA+, gathered in one Command Center.

How it's built: I design every build as the instructor, from the objectives it covers to the scenarios, the hints and the accessibility rules, and I test it against what my students actually need. The code is written with AI-assisted development (Claude Code) under my direction, and new builds are tested in a real browser, against their objectives, before students see them.

  • Objectives firstEvery question and lab is filed to the current exam objectives, and each topic carries at least 20 questions.
  • Built for every readerThe program's standard is WCAG AAA contrast, checked on the rendered page, with dyslexia-friendly reading, because many of my students carry vision damage from their service.
  • Hints that guideUnlimited tries, hints that point to the evidence instead of the answer, and wrong options that are real near misses.
