## The Cyber Warrior Program

The study platform I design for my students: more than 35 free, interactive study sites across A+, Network+, Security+ and CySA+, gathered in one **[Command Center](https://rafikiscyent888.github.io/Cyber-Warrior-Command-Center-2.0/)**.

**How it's built:** I design every build as the instructor, from the objectives it covers to the scenarios, the hints and the accessibility rules. The code is written with AI-assisted development (Claude Code) under my direction, and new builds are tested in a real browser, against their objectives, before students see them. The program's standard is WCAG AAA contrast and dyslexia-friendly reading, because many of my students carry vision damage from their service.

## How I teach

How does a student get past memorizing and into troubleshooting? With a steady foundation first, then complexity added one layer at a time, so every new idea is anchored to one they already own.

**Foundation first. Then build.**

That's how the fundamentals stop being trivia and start being skill.
